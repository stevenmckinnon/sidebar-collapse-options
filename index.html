Collapsed sidebar: four ways to handle pages

Follow-up: option B when group names clash →

Each mock is live. It starts collapsed; use the panel icon at the top of the rail to expand it, and click pages to switch. The truncated labels ("Overv…", "Positi…") are gone in all four, because a 52px rail can't hold a readable page name.

A. One Pages button, full list in a flyout My pick

The rail shows a single pages button. Clicking it opens the complete grouped list at full width.

Good
  • Rail height stays fixed however many pages exist
  • Full names and groups, nothing abbreviated
  • Works with keyboard and touch
Watch out
  • Switching pages is two clicks
  • Current page name is only visible in the canvas header

B. One chip per group

Each group becomes a two-letter chip with a page count. Hover or click a chip to see its pages.

Good
  • Group structure stays visible when collapsed
  • The chip for the current group is highlighted
  • Shorter flyouts than A
Watch out
  • Initials collide ("Analytics" and "Admin")
  • Rail grows with the number of groups
  • Needs a fallback for ungrouped pages (the page-icon chip)

C. Hover to peek

The rail shows a dot per page as a position marker. Hovering the rail slides the full sidebar over the canvas without moving the layout.

Good
  • No second design to learn; collapsed is the same sidebar
  • Fastest with a mouse: move, click, leave
  • Dots show where you are and how big the report is
Watch out
  • Opens by accident when the pointer crosses the rail to reach an action icon
  • Needs a tap alternative on touch screens
  • Covers the left edge of the canvas while open

D. Move page switching to the canvas header

The rail holds actions only. A switcher in the canvas header shows the current group and page, with previous and next.

Good
  • Current page name is always visible
  • Previous and next make stepping through a report one click
  • Simplest rail of the four
Watch out
  • Page navigation lives in two places depending on sidebar state
  • Takes a row of canvas height if there is no header today
