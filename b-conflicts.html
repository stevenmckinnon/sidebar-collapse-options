Recommendation

Combine 1 and 2. When someone creates a group, generate a unique label for it and save it with the group, so it never changes when other groups are added or reordered. Let people change the label or pick an icon from the group's menu, and warn them if their label is already used.

Colour (rail 3) works as an extra cue on top of that, but not as the only difference between two chips, since some people can't tell the colours apart.
